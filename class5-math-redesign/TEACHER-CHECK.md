## 3. Our choices (not the book's slips)

- **Drawn true to the numbers:** where the book prints a measure (an angle, a length, a share, a count) but draws it loosely, our drawing follows the number: e.g. pp. 34 and 48 (tiles, the floor), p. 152–153 (the 40° and 45° angles), p. 163 (the 70° parallelogram), pp. 174–175 (bar heights from the tables), pp. 178–182 (pie slices at share × 360°). The book's words are unchanged.
- **Read from a small picture, please check against the answer key:** p. 102's gallery (55 spectators on 100 seats); p. 146's eight clocks (read at 700 dpi: 3:35, 7:55, 10:25, 3:58, 10:02, 12:20, 1:40, 6:15; the 4th and 5th could also be 4:00 and 10:00, the 6th 1:20); p. 178's grocer's pie (no numbers printed: slices measured as about 23%, 12%, 40% and 25%).
- **Space:** p. 128's dot paper has 15 × 7 dots (the book 18 × 8), because a true-size grid can't be shrunk to fit the page.
- **Review numbers** print «১.» (our series' style) where the book prints «১।».
- **Pages scaled to fit** (the fit step, at least 85%): listed by `node build.js`.
