### Exercises that do not fit cleanly: what a new kind or variant would need

1. **Number line 0–1** (p96–97 অনু ৭, p97 অনু ৮, and the 0–1 line under the p97 fraction wall).
   - Show a 0–1 line cut into d equal intervals.
   - **Read:** type the fraction for each tick, or for the end of a coloured bar.
   - **Place:** tap the tick for n/d.
   - Add a "where is d/d?" question, where the answer is the tick at 1.
   - The line should carry a unit label ("মিটার"), because the book uses "১ মিটার".
   - This is the biggest gap. Neither shade nor name it covers reading positions.
2. **Equivalent or not?** (p99 অনু ১০, কাজ ৪, p106 নিজে ৬, ~14 items).
   - The choices are two buttons, "সমতুল" and "সমতুল নয়".
   - It should have an optional cross-multiplication scaffold: two boxes for d1×n2 and n1×d2, shown with crossed arrows exactly as on p98.
   - This could be a variant of compare (a = vs ≠ judgement) or of same amount.
3. **Many equivalents / any valid answer** (p99 অনু ১১, কাজ ৩, p105 নিজে ৫).
   - Show a row of k blanks.
   - Accept any n·m / d·m. Reject duplicates and the original fraction.
   - Optionally use the scaffold (n×□)/(d×□) from p98 কাজ ২, which has a blank multiplier.
   - Same amount as planned has one fixed answer, so it needs this "open answer" checking.
4. **Fraction wall** (p97 কাজ ১, p97 অনু ৯).
   - Show stacked equal bars split into 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 parts, with the same length shaded.
   - The child types each fraction.
   - This can be a multi-row same amount round, if same amount can show more than one bar.
5. **Compare with words and true/false** (p94 অনু ২, অনু ৩).
   - The answer buttons are বড়ো / সমান / ছোটো instead of >, <, =.
   - p94 অনু ৩ is a "Mina says 1/2 is smaller than 1/4: right or wrong?" item.
   - These are cheap variants of compare: button labels plus a statement-check mode. Also note p99 অনু ১২ (ঘ) compares a whole number ১ with 6/7.
6. **Unequal-looking partitions and more shapes** (p92 অনু ২, p93 কাজ ১, p104 MCQ 1).
   - Shade has to support triangles, ellipses, rhombuses, a pentagon, diagonal cuts, and the 8-triangle square.
   - It also has to support pieces of different sizes, where the child must pick the piece that is 1/4. Examples are the rectangle in 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/4 and the triangle-in-rectangle.
   - It needs a "no line drawn" mode where the child first draws the halving line (the pentagon), which is cut equally with a free cut.
   - p104 MCQ 1 adds **"name the unshaded part"**, which is a flag for name it.
7. **Find লব/হর** (p96 অনু ৫: tap the লব, as with △, and tap the হর, as with ○).
   - **Build from words** (p96 অনু ৬, p105 নিজে ৩: "লব ৭, হর ১৩", sometimes given হর first).
   - These are very small variants of name it that need no picture.
8. **Three-way matching** (p105 নিজে ২, নিজে ৪).
   - Match fraction ↔ picture ↔ words ("পাঁচ ভাগের তিন ভাগ").
   - p105 নিজে ২ mixes answers of different types: 6/5, the word "সমতুল ভগ্নাংশ", and ১.
   - The existing Matching activity type may already cover this. Otherwise Fraction Play needs a "match" round.
9. **Story problems with units** (14 items).
   - The add and take away rounds need a story line and a unit after the answer: মিটার, লিটার, ঘণ্টা, কিলোমিটার, অংশ.
   - Two items need **two answers**, a name and an amount: "কে বেশি … এবং কত অংশ বেশি" (p103 কাজ ৩, অনু ৪, and p106 নিজে ১০).
   - The opposite-directions distance problems (p101 অনু ৪, p104 MCQ 7, p106 নিজে ৯) are additions.
10. **Pick the operation** (p104 MCQ 3: 8/9 □ 3/9 = 5/9, choose + − × ÷). This is a small add / take away variant.
11. **Sums above 1** (p105 নিজে ২: 3/5 + 3/5 = 6/5).
    - Add must allow an answer of n > d and draw two wholes.
    - Otherwise the book's sums stay ≤ 1. The answer n/n should be accepted as "১", and vice versa, because the book writes "5/5 = ১".
12. **Plain MCQ** (p104, 8 items). The options are fractions or fraction lists, so a generic "choose the right one" round would carry them as they are.
13. **Free drawing / explaining** (p94 অনু ৩ "কেন?", p94 অনু ৪ "draw two shapes"). These can't be checked as written. Use true/false instead of "explain why", and use two shade rounds instead of free drawing.

**Answer formats the book uses:**
- fraction boxes (□ over □)
- the 3-step scaffold (□/□ − □/□ = (□ − □)/□ = □/□)
- a tick in a table
- a ✓ next to ক/খ/গ/ঘ
- drawing lines between columns

The book never reduces fractions. It writes 2/4, 4/6, 6/8, 4/8 and 6/9 as they are. **The game should accept the unreduced answer and should not require simplest form.**
