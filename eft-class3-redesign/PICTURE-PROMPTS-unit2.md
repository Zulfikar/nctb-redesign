I'm illustrating a new edition of an English textbook for Class Three children (age 8) in Bangladesh. I'll ask for one picture at a time and give you reference sheets for the characters. Please keep everything below in every picture.

**The style:**
- A bright, friendly 2D children's book illustration: clean dark outlines, soft shading, warm cheerful colours, rounded shapes.
- Simple, uncluttered backgrounds that still show the place clearly: a Bangladeshi primary school, its classrooms and its grounds.
- Landscape, 3:2 (1536 × 1024), PNG.

**The people:**
- The children are 8 years old, Bangladeshi, friendly and happy, in modest clothes.
- **The school uniform:**
  - girls wear a blue dress with a white collar and a white belt;
  - boys wear a white short-sleeved shirt and blue shorts;
  - everyone wears white socks and white canvas shoes.
- Use the reference sheets I upload for every named character: the same face, hair, glasses, hijab and so on.

**Never write anything.** No text, letters, words or numbers anywhere: not on the blackboard, books, posters, signs, clothes or bags.
- A wall clock has dots instead of numbers.
- Posters show only simple pictures (a boat, flowers, a landscape).
- The Bangladesh flag is fine (green with a red circle).

**Speech bubbles are added later by us.** When I ask for space for bubbles, keep that part of the picture calm (plain sky, wall or blackboard) with no faces in it. Don't draw any bubbles yourself.
