## 4. The scenes

### `u4-l1-den.png` (page 50, the rhyme: "A den and a hen / Ten men look into / A tiger's den. / They see no tiger, / But a fat red hen.")
**Upload:** none.

**The picture:** a funny moment at the mouth of a big rocky cave in the forest, the tiger's den. **Ten village men** (different ages: some young, one with a grey beard; lungis and shirts, one with a *gamchha* on his shoulder, one holding a stick) crowd around the opening and lean in to peek, nervous and then surprised. Inside the den, in the light, there is **no tiger, only a fat red hen**, sitting proudly and looking back at them. Green trees around the cave. Landscape 3:2. The page shows it beside the rhyme, whole.

### `u4-l3-boat.png` (page 55, beside the rhyme "Row, Row, Row Your Boat")
**Upload:** `ref-children-1.png`.

**The picture:** **portrait, 2:3 (1024 × 1536).** A sunny river in Bangladesh. **Faria** (two short pigtails with white bows) and **Rahat** (curly hair, round glasses) sit in a small wooden rowing boat and row gently down the stream, smiling, in their school uniform. The rhyme's three animals are there, friendly and funny: a **crocodile**'s eyes and snout peeking out of the water near the boat, a **lion** standing on the riverbank roaring, and far away a **polar bear** on a small floating block of ice, shivering. Blue water with gentle waves, green banks, a few white clouds. The page shows it tall and narrow beside the rhyme, whole.

### `u4-l6-months.png` (page 62, B: "January is the first month of the year." … "December is the twelfth month of the year")
**Upload:** none.

**The picture:** one long, continuous landscape of a year in a Bangladeshi village, its seasons flowing from left to right:
- **winter** (left): misty morning, a field of yellow mustard flowers, a man carrying clay pots of date-palm juice on a pole over his shoulder;
- **spring and summer**: trees in blossom, a mango tree full of ripe mangoes;
- **monsoon** (middle): rain, two children laughing under a big banana leaf as an umbrella, a full green pond;

Landscape 3:2, but **the page shows only a wide strip across the middle** (about 172 × 38 mm): keep every season's main things in **a band across the middle 40% of the height**, with plain sky above and grass below.
