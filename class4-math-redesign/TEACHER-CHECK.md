## 3. Where our page differs from the book on purpose
- **Drawings are true to what the text says.** The book's figures are often stretched (its «৬০°» on p১৪৫ measures 53°, «৩০°» 24°; its squares on p১৬৫ are squashed; a right angle is 88°; the isosceles and equilateral triangles of pp১৫৭–১৬৮ have uneven sides). Ours are drawn exactly: a named angle at its size, equal sides equal, squares square. An angle the children must measure is drawn to the nearest 5° of the book's.
- **True sizes:** the rulers (pp১১৩, ১২১), the 1 cm squared paper (pp১৩২–১৩৩), the 4 cm square (p১৩৪), the parallel lines 16 mm apart (p১৫২). p১১৩'s red ribbon is drawn 7.4 cm, the length its questions build up to (the book's is about 7.3 cm).
- **Answer boxes never give the answer away:** p২৭ (৪)–(৬) get 7 boxes, as on p২৬ (the book prints 8).
- **p৫০:** the book's broken reph «পূবের্র» is written «পূর্বের».
- **p৩৯:** the book prints a hyphen for minus; ours is «−».
- **p১৩৮:** a stray «6» beside the second clock is left out.
- **p১৬৯ (please check):** the zoo picture is too blurred to count, so the pens were drawn with জিরাফ ২, বাঘ ৩, হাতি ৪ (the table's own number), সিংহ ৫, জেব্রা ৬ and হরিণ ৯, so that "most" and "fewest" each have one answer.
